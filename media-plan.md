1. **Source resolution is low.** Everything came through WhatsApp/Facebook: images are 526–2048px and videos are 478×850 portrait. Nothing will be upscaled. The home hero (1280px source) will look a little soft on large desktop screens. If the client has the original camera files, send those later and the same slots can be re-run.
2. **No burglar-proofing photos.** Under "remove unfilled slots", the Burglar Proofing page loses its gallery, and its home service card becomes a plain navy card with no photo.
3. **No gutter close-ups and no "charcoal gutters + charcoal fascia + charcoal garage door" photo.** The only charcoal-fascia source is video 30. For the charcoal tile in the Matched Finishes section, I've proposed a still from that video (row V2). The alternative is photo 22 (charcoal garage doors, but stainless gutters).
4. **Videos are portrait (9:16).** I'll switch the video player to a portrait frame so they aren't letterboxed in a wide box.
5. **Audio will be stripped from all 3 videos.** I can't verify there's useful narration. Tell me if any should keep sound.
6. **Flyers 05, 06, 07, 09 carry different contact details** from the ones on this site. They may belong to another business or be out of date. None of the flyers are used on the site either way.
7. **File 25 is a phone screenshot** (status bar and nav bar visible) of a house with stainless pillars and balustrades. It's low quality and may not be the client's own photo. It's marked unusable unless you confirm it's their project.
8. Photos 14, 17 and 19 show building rubble or installers mid-job. I've put them in the Projects gallery and service galleries only, never in hero or home slots. Photo 19 has a "TECNO SPARK 10" camera watermark, which I'll crop off.
9. The site has no separate "Folding doors" section. Folding-door photos are saved in `images/folding-doors/` but appear under **Aluminium Doors & Windows** on the site.
